=== HERO POSITIONING (How It Works) ===
"How FanCup works" — "Predict AI football matches with FUN credits, then follow kickoff, live
play, and settlement from one match screen."

01 - INTRO — "FUN-only football predictions"
"FanCup is a play-money prediction game. Connect your wallet, pick a market on an AI-simulated
football match, and stake FUN credits. Nation fan tokens are shown as team collectibles, not
staking tokens."

02 - MATCH FLOW — "Receive FUN - predict - settle"
- Receive FUN — every new wallet receives play-money FUN credits once on signup.
- Choose a market — pick 1X2 or Over/Under on the headline AI 6v6 fixture.
- Stake FUN — predictions use FUN only; nation tokens are view-only.
- Lock before kickoff — confirmed picks lock; the stake moves from available to locked.
- Settle in FUN — winning picks return stake x locked odds in play-money credits.

03 - AI MATCH ENGINE — "Every match is played by trained AI teams"
"FanCup matches are not scripted outcomes. Each nation is represented by an AI team model with
its own tendencies: passing rhythm, pressure, transition speed, compactness, shooting urgency and
tactical style. Once kickoff starts, those models play the match inside the simulation."
- Model-driven teams — every nation has its own decision tendencies.
- Live simulation — teams resolve passes, shots, pressure and counters after kickoff.

04 - VRF FAIRNESS — "True randomness with VRF"
"Before the simulation starts, FanCup requests verifiable randomness through MagicBlock VRF. That
randomness becomes the match seed. The seed drives the deterministic simulation: which choices
open up, how pressure moments resolve, when teams commit to a pass, shot, press or counter. The
same seed and same team models reproduce the same match, so the game is fair and auditable."
- Verifiable seed — MagicBlock VRF supplies the unpredictable match seed.
- Reproducible result — same seed plus same models produces the same match.
